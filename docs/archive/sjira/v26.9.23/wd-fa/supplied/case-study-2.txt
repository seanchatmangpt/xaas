06 · GROUNDING AND TRUST
Four guards against a confident wrong root cause
P1 · P2
1
Citation or nothing
Each claim links a source span + timestamp (2e). Uncited claims are removed.
2
Applicability, not probability
Engineer sees “4 of 5 met” with the missing condition named (2d).
3
Stated falsifier
“T17 > 40 → not FM-217” is on the card, before any action (P2).
4
UNKNOWN is allowed
Missing, stale or conflicting evidence blocks a recommendation (2h).
Human in the loop:
every write, every escalation, every novel-mode call. Auto-close only at 5 / 5 with 1-in-10 sampling.

09 · FEEDBACK
Feedback without extra work
P2
DISPOSITION = LABEL
Closing, reopening or routing a case already records the verdict. Nothing extra to fill in.
CALL RECORD
Each auto-close names rule, evidence and a spot-checker. A reopen demotes the rule.
CREDITED RULES
A senior call (“always T17 on line 3”) becomes rule R-044, with its hit rate shown.
Weekly: retrain reranker
On reopen: suspend rule
Monthly: review proposed rules
10 · SECURITY AND GOVERNANCE
Security and governance
P1 · 2a · 2h
ACL propagation
Source ACLs copied onto every chunk; filtered at retrieval, not at display.
Access denied state
Shown with who can grant access, never silently omitted (2h).
IP and PII

Auditability
Receipt per action: identity, authority, consequence, replay, standing (P4 · L4).
11 · EVALUATION
Evaluation plan
BEFORE LAUNCH
Golden set: 300 closed cases replayed blind, time-sliced to prevent leakage
Adversarial set: look-alike modes, stale docs, conflicting lots
4-week shadow mode alongside engineers
AFTER LAUNCH
Reopen rate and engineer override rate, per rule
UNKNOWN rate: a rise means data pipelines are failing
Golden set re-run on every model, prompt or rule change
