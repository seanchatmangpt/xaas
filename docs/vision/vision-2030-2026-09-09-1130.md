**Real, grounded finding** (disclosed by cycle 1030, re-confirmed live just now):
`config/config.exs`'s `:xaas, :ex4pm_ontology_check` config is broken by
construction:

```elixir
config :xaas, :ex4pm_ontology_check,
  repo_path: System.get_env("EX4PM_REPO_PATH", Path.expand("~/ex4pm")),
  pinned_sha: "18c6d531c3ac42aee51ba4ddf1daf280f4864717",
  upstream_path: "lib/ex4pm/ocel.ex",
  vendored_path: "priv/packs/xaas_library_pack/ontology.ttl"
```

Two independent real defects:

1. `vendored_path` points at the WRONG pack — `priv/packs/xaas_library_pack/
   ontology.ttl` is the unrelated Library-domain ontology (books/curations,
   `bibo:`/`schema:`/`org:` prefixes), not this session's telemetry/OCEL-envelope
   ontology (`priv/packs/xaas_telemetry_pack/ontology.ttl`, which grounds
   `Ex4pm.OCEL.validate_envelope/1`'s field shape).
2. Even corrected to the right path, `upstream_path: "lib/ex4pm/ocel.ex"` is a real
   Elixir source file (~400 lines of code), and `vendored_path` is a hand-authored
   RDF/Turtle grounding of a SUBSET of that file's fields — these are never
   byte-identical by construction (one transcribes/grounds the other's semantics,
   it is not a literal copy). `Xaas.Ontology.Ex4pmStaleness`'s own moduledoc states
   its actual, correct design intent: byte-identical comparison of "our vendored
   copy and the pinned upstream ex4pm source it was **copied from**" — but no such
   literal-copy relationship exists between these two specific files. This check
   has likely never produced a real `{:ok, :match}` since it was introduced.
