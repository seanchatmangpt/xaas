## Is Toyota part of the WD case?

No. Toyota/TPS is used only as a reference structure for quality-system concepts such as standards, abnormality, work, disposition, verification and continuous improvement.

## Is STOGAF official TOGAF?

No. STOGAF is this project's TOGAF-compatible semantic execution profile.

## Is the demo using WD data?

No. The evidence ceiling is repository-local fixture data.

## Is the AI authorized to close FA cases?

No. Friday authority is SELECT_CONSTRUCT_ONLY and consequential FA disposition remains ENGINEER_DISPOSITION_REQUIRED.

## Is the alternate FastAPI/Next.js stack the architecture?

No. It is intended to be a generated projection of the same canonical semantics.

## Why so much formalism?

Because the difficult part of enterprise AI is not producing plausible text. It is preserving identity, evidence, authority, reproducibility and organizational learning across systems and teams.
