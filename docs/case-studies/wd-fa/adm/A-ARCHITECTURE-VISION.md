# Phase A — Architecture Vision

## Vision

Every verified failure investigation should permanently increase WD's ability to resolve the next comparable failure with less human investigative work.

## Stakeholder value

- FA engineer: starts with what requires judgment.
- FA manager: sees blockers, missing evidence and recurrence.
- platform/data: gets explicit source and lineage contracts.
- security/governance: gets inspectable evidence and authority boundaries.
- executive: gets measurable process outcomes rather than AI activity metrics.

## Scope

In scope: triage, evidence, prior art, next action, known/novel, human disposition, verification, learning, evaluation and staged deployment.

Out of scope for Friday: live WD data, production deployment, production DO, measured MTTR improvement.

## Success hypothesis

A governed semantic FA loop can reduce repeated reconstruction and exploratory work without increasing false-known risk.
