# Live Q&A Bank

## Why not just RAG?

RAG can retrieve evidence but does not by itself define applicability, authority, work state, verification or organizational learning. Retrieval is one capability inside the operating loop.

## Why not let the LLM choose the root cause?

The system allows models to rank candidates. KNOWN standing requires deterministic applicability and evidence closure. This separates plausibility from admitted engineering knowledge.

## Why OCEL?

Failure-analysis events relate simultaneously to case, drive, lot, supplier, firmware, station and evidence artifacts. OCEL retains those object relationships without forcing them into one case-centric event stream.

## Why Semantic Jira?

The next action is not only prose; it is an obligation with subject, owner, required evidence, dependencies and standing. sJira is that work projection.

## Why SA2A?

Capabilities are typed and bounded. Retrieval, ranking, applicability, work construction and verification can compose without granting ambient authority.

## Why keep a human gate?

The Friday evidence does not establish production actuation policy. The proposal therefore keeps consequential FA disposition with the engineer and treats broader authority as a later policy/evidence decision.

## What does MachineExperience add?

It turns a verified novel investigation into applicability-bounded prior art that can reduce future exploratory work. It is stronger than simply adding the report to a vector database.

## What would you do in the first week at WD?

Inventory exact source systems, identities, ACLs and ground-truth process; select one bounded failure family; assemble representative positive/partial/novel cases; establish baseline process measures; and agree on evidence/authority boundaries before integrating broadly.

## What is the most important metric?

False-KNOWN is the critical trust guardrail. The business outcome metrics then include time to disposition, exploratory steps, engineer touches, prior-art reuse and MTTR.

## What is actually implemented?

Repository-local deterministic triage mechanics, object-centric evidence, browser behavior, STOGAF architecture projection and fixture replay are implemented/courted. Real WD source integration, production deployment and measured MTTR are not claimed.
