# Evidence Ceiling and Non-Claims

The following statements are intentionally **not** made by this repository-local demo:

- We do not have access to WD production data.
- We do not claim access to WD Confluence, Jira, data lake, MES, QMS or IAM.
- We do not claim the synthetic fixture distribution represents production.
- We do not claim production root-cause accuracy.
- We do not claim production false-KNOWN or false-UNKNOWN rates.
- We do not claim measured WD MTTR reduction.
- We do not claim production deployment.
- We do not claim production actuation authority.
- We do not claim a customer-approved ontology.
- We do not claim ST-7 ACTUATED, ST-8 CLOSED_LOOP or ST-9 LEARNING.
- We do not claim Toyota is a WD customer or part of Case Study 2.
- We do not claim STOGAF is an official Open Group specification.

## What the demo may claim after green exact-head courts

The repository may claim only the mechanics actually observed at the exact head, including deterministic fixture admission, object-centric persistence, bounded authority, browser behavior and verified replay where those courts pass.
