AI/ML trade-off

Division of labour

Deterministic ranker owns Candidate generation from circulation history Scoring, ordering, and tie-breaking Availability, reading-level, and opt-out filters Reproducibility — same inputs, same list
Language model owns Explaining a ranked title in a student's own history Enriching thin catalog records for librarian review Translating librarian questions into catalog queries Never selecting, ordering, or admitting a title
If the model is unavailable, students still get ranked recommendations — they just arrive without the sentence explaining them.
Edge cases we own

Cold start and thin metadata

New student No history. We start from the grade-band cohort and three interest taps, then decay the cohort weight as their own record fills in. usable list from checkout one
Thin record A MARC record with no summary gets model-proposed subjects and level. It sits in a review queue and cannot influence ranking until a librarian approves it. human approval gate, always
New title Content and semantic signals only until it has five circulations, so a new arrival is never buried by having no history. graduates into collaborative scoring

Realtime

One curation, six hops, no refresh

01 Librarian pins a title LiveView event
02 Ash action runs Policy check, changeset, validation
03 Postgres commits Curation row is durable
04 Notifier fires After commit — never before
05 PubSub broadcast Topic per student and per school
06 Student view patches Diff over the socket, no reload
Why it matters A broadcast can never describe a write that failed to land.
Same path, reversed A student checkout patches the librarian dashboard through the identical six hops.
Governance

Where student data stops

Decision Our position
Identifiers Internal student ids only. Names never leave the district boundary.
LLM payloads Title, subject, and reading level. No student name, id, or borrowing history.
Retention Recommendation logs 180 days, then aggregate. Explanations regenerate; they are not archived.
Access Librarian scoped to their school, admin to aggregates. Enforced by Ash policies at the resource, not the page.
Opt-out Per student, honored at candidate generation — not filtered at display time.
Audit Every recommendation stores its weights, candidate pool, and model version.
Reading records are among the most protected data a school holds. We treat FERPA and COPPA as design constraints, not a compliance review at the end.

Risk register

What could go wrong, and our answer

Risk Position
Sparse circulation data Content and cohort signals carry the cold start; librarian pinning covers the gap from day one.
Model invents a book It cannot. Candidates come from the catalog index; the model only writes prose about admitted titles.
ILS integration slips Nightly CSV or SIP2 ingest is the fallback path, scoped in week 1 so the pilot date holds.
Equity of recommendations Availability and format weights favour what students can actually borrow; subgroup outcomes reviewed at week 9.
Staff adoption The product ships curation controls, not automation. The librarian keeps the last word, visibly.
Vendor lock-in The Ash domain is portable Elixir. Hosting and model provider are swappable at the boundary.
