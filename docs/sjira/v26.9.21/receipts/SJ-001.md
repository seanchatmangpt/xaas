## The defect and the fix

Defect (reproduced by two independent verifiers on `b64f17b`): `SemanticWork.admit/1` accepted a
descriptor whose `graph_digest` was altered after admission when the optional `admission_digest`
envelope was omitted (probe B) or altered together with `graph_digest` (probe C). The envelope is
a second in-band copy, so it can never be the guard, and the real emitter never sends it.

Fix: `Xaas.Ultracode.SemanticWork.AdmissionBinding`, called from `SemanticWork.admit/2`.
XaaS recomputes the digest from material the tamperer did not also rewrite:

- `admitted_work_order` (new optional descriptor key): the admitted snapshot the graph side
  returned. XaaS recomputes its `work_order_digest` and its `definition_digest` from the content
  and requires the descriptor's `base_sha`, `repository_identity`, `work_order_iri` and the
  bridge's identity, repository, base_sha and definition_digest to be the snapshot's.
- `bridge.source_snapshot_digest`: the real bridge's per-work-order digest, a second anchor.
- All anchors, and the envelope when present, must agree. A `binding:` option, declared by the
  trusted caller and never by the descriptor, decides whether `graph_digest` itself is bound:
  `:snapshot` (required, with at least one independent anchor, else `:admission_anchor_missing`),
  `:graph` (graph-wide digest, unbound) or `:auto` (default; `:snapshot` when a snapshot is
  carried). `mix xaas.semantic.materialize` takes `--binding auto|snapshot|graph`.
- Envelope-carrying callers stay compatible: the envelope check and its typed reasons are
  unchanged.

The recomputation reproduces the real graph digests byte for byte on the real descriptor
(`work_order_digest` `4e04589...`, `definition_digest`
`sha256:aa8e146cf922f53f6008d716b3da0955040d8187509383e8ecabff9d339217ab`).

Pre-fix versus post-fix, same real descriptor, run in one process (pre-fix module compiled from
`sjira/sj-001:lib/xaas/ultracode/semantic_work.ex`, post-fix with `binding: :snapshot`):

| probe | pre-fix | post-fix |
|---|---|---|
| untampered | ACCEPTED | ACCEPTED |
| A: `graph_digest` altered, envelope kept | refused (`admission_digest_mismatch`) | refused (`admission_digest_mismatch`) |
| B: `graph_digest` altered, envelope omitted | ACCEPTED | refused (`graph_digest_unbound`) |
| C: `graph_digest` and envelope altered together | ACCEPTED | refused (`admission_anchor_disagree`) |
