For each of the 41 Art 9/13/14 lines classified OPEN_GAP by W523's residual rule,
judged at the SYSTEM level: this surface is a **deployer-class system** (it builds and
operates its own internal high-risk-adjacent machinery; it does not place products on
the market, engage notified bodies, or run provider conformity procedures). Each line
was reclassified as:

- **EVIDENCED(path + receipt verdict)** — a real on-disk seam partially or fully
  satisfies the obligation; the test asserts path existence AND the receipt's verdict
  line (`String.contains?` on the receipt body).
- **NOT_APPLICABLE(reason)** — the duty is addressed to a role we do not occupy
  (provider placing-on-market, authority), is a connector/definitional clause whose
  operative content is classified separately, or is a permissive "may" clause.
- **OPEN_GAP** — kept only where the obligation genuinely applies to us and no seam
  exists.
